# Asmita

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Survivors in India seeking help with image-based abuse come first. They may be on a slow connection, using a small phone or need to leave a screen quickly. The site also serves people helping someone, minors who need a separate support route, and organizations and platforms responding to reports.

## Product Purpose

The public site helps people understand image-based abuse, reach appropriate support and file or track a complaint. English and Hindi are supported.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The filing flow handles links as text, generates fingerprints on the device and requires review and signing before the ordinary notice is sent.

## Product Principles

Lead with the action a visitor came to take. Explain what the service can do and what happens next. Ask for only the data the chosen action requires.
