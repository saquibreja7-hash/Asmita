# Partner applications: review guidance

> Draft: not reviewed. Written from the platform-partners code and the
> partners console; it carries no legal advice and nothing in it replaces
> the owner's word.

## What this queue holds

Applications from platforms that want to work with Asmita — take-down
cooperation, fingerprint alerts, or a partner feed. The card shows the
application's own fields: platform, website, organisation, size, contact
and what they asked for.

## Deciding

- **Mark contacted** records that you have replied, and keeps the
  application open for a real decision.
- **Approve application** onboards the platform as a partner with the
  interests they asked for.
- **Decline application** closes the application.

## Good practice

Check the contact details against the platform's own published channels
before approving; the directory of platforms and their verified contacts is
on the Platforms page. A decision here only changes the partner record —
nothing is sent to the applicant automatically, so telling them the outcome
is a separate, human step.
