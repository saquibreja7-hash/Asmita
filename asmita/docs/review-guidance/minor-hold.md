# Possible minor: review guidance

> Draft: not reviewed. Written from the crawler runbook and the console's
> own rules; it carries no legal advice and nothing in it replaces the
> owner's word.

## What this queue holds

Findings labelled **Possible minor**. Only a SUPER_ADMIN sees this queue,
and nothing in it continues the adult flow: no decision is offered here, and
no rule or hand can move an item out.

## What happens to an item here

- The label is a safety switch, not a note. While it is set, the finding
  cannot be approved or dismissed, and no representative notice can be
  prepared or sent for it.
- The only way back to the adult queue is the existing two-admin flow:
  a **different** admin clears the label with a written reason, recorded on
  the finding and in the audit log. The sync then moves the subject back to
  its own queue.

## What to do

Follow the interim minors handling for crawler findings and tell the owner
straight away; the written POCSO procedure is still a launch gate, and until
it exists this state is the minors handling.
