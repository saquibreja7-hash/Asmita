# Crawler findings: review guidance

> Draft: not reviewed. Written from the findings console's own interim
> safety note and the crawler runbook; it carries no legal advice and
> nothing in it replaces the owner's word.

## Before you look

- A match is a lead, not proof. Distance and quality only say two images
  look alike.
- Do not open the address on a personal device or personal account. Use
  only the equipment set aside for this work.
- This console never opens, loads or previews the address for you. You see
  it as text only, behind the reasoned, recorded reveal, limited to twenty
  an hour.
- If the page appears to involve a child: stop, do not save, copy or share
  anything, label the finding **Possible minor** (that stops the adult flow
  for it), and tell the SUPER_ADMIN straight away.

## Deciding

- A close match is decided by one analyst; a distant or SSCD match above
  distance 20 needs a second, different analyst before the approval counts.
- **Approve finding** sends nothing by itself: an approved finding later
  supports a representative notice, prepared and sent as her authorised
  representative under her crawl authorisation, with its own preview and
  its own dispatch gate. Her signature is never copied onto it.
- **Dismiss finding** closes the match with your internal reason.
- **Label Possible minor** moves the finding out of this queue at once,
  into the protected Possible minor queue that only a SUPER_ADMIN sees.

## Consent scope

The card says which authorisation the finding was matched under: a live
crawl authorisation, a watch-only authorisation (she has not asked for
removal yet, and nothing can be sent), or a withdrawn one (no notice can be
sent for this finding).
