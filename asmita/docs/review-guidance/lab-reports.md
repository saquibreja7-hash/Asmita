# Lab reports: review guidance

> Draft: not reviewed. Written from the AI Lab charter and the Lab reports
> page; it carries no legal advice and nothing in it replaces the owner's
> word.

## What this queue holds

Draft abuse reports from the AI Lab: a report a Lab analyst has already
written about a nudify service or a chokepoint provider, waiting to be
sent. This is Lab work, not survivor complaint work; the queue machinery is
shared, nothing else is.

## Deciding

- **Send by email** sends the draft through the Lab's own email sender, to
  the chokepoint's stored abuse contact. The address must be an email
  address; if it is not, the console refuses and the report is sent from the
  AI Lab page, where the address is typed in.
- **Record web-form submission** records that you submitted the report
  through the provider's own web form; the stored text becomes the record,
  exactly as on the AI Lab reports page.

## Good practice

Read the draft on the card before sending: it names the service and the
provider it goes to. The Lab keeps its own record of outcomes; recording
what the provider did afterwards happens on the Lab reports page.
