No UI in this WP, so no page renders yet; nothing else in the app imports these modules yet, and no crawler or complaint-workflow file was touched.

1. `Effort` gained a "none" value. Part D gives awesome-safety-tools "Effort: none" but the Part D interface allows only low/medium/high; the data says what is true, and WP151's card can render zero filled squares for it. roost-model-community gets "none" too (a community to read, like its sibling; Part D states it only for awesome-safety-tools).
2. Part D's dual effort ratings collapsed to one value each: vpdq "low to medium" became medium (its effortNote says it is close to PDQ's low effort if you already run ffmpeg); sscd "medium to high" became high; c2pa "low to verify, medium to sign" became medium (the main audience, AI apps, signs). take-it-down and asmita-fingerprint-feed get medium (join, then match), matching StopNCII's stated platform effort.
3. Part D gives no audiences for vpdq and tmk-pdqf; vpdq inherits PDQ's three (platform, ngo, researcher), tmk-pdqf gets platform and researcher.
4. The two directories' `related` lists (Part D gives none): awesome-safety-tools points to roost-model-community; roost-model-community points to awesome-safety-tools and its two listed partner models, ncii-prompt-guard and gpt-oss-safeguard (a Part D fact).
