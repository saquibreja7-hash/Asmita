# E3: morph-to-source tracing

Charter: `asmita/docs/lab/charter.md` (experiment E3). Tool: the public page
`/lab/trace` and the library under `src/lib/lab/trace/` (WP76, branch
`feat/lab-trace`); harness command `tools/lab-bench trace` (the E1 set
format). This card holds the method, the thresholds and how they were chosen,
the metric, and the aggregate results table; a real run's numbers are filled
in below and nowhere else. Methodology and aggregate numbers only — no
per-image data, no service names, no real photos.

**Question.** Can we show automatically that a morph was made from her photo?

**Status.** Tool built, unit-tested and exercised in a real browser with
synthetic images (identical and unrelated pairs, 2026-11-21). No real run has
happened yet: the table below stays empty until one does. A real run needs
the E1 test set (built locally from clothed, non-intimate photos of
consenting adults) and belongs to local disk, never this repository.

## Method

The analysis runs entirely in the visitor's browser (Cloudflare Workers
cannot run the ONNX model or native image libraries): the images never leave
the device, and the page says so. Both the page and the lab-bench `trace`
command run the same pure scoring core (`src/lib/lab/trace/score.ts`), so a
harness number and a page number mean the same thing.

Per suspect/original pair, both images decoded to RGBA and capped at a 2048
long-edge working copy (never upscaled; both sides through the same
pipeline):

1. **Whole image** — PDQ (the site's own `src/lib/pdq/pdq.ts`, the exact
   hash survivors' browsers compute), hamming distance 0..256.
2. **Face region** — PDQ of the located face box plus a 30% margin (E1's
   `FACE_CROP_MARGIN`); skipped when no face is found on either side or the
   crop is under 48 px (E1's `MIN_FACE_CROP_SIDE`).
3. **Background region** — PDQ with the face box plus a 15% margin masked
   out in flat grey 128 (E1's `BACKGROUND_MASK_MARGIN`/`MASK_GREY`): a morph
   typically keeps one region and replaces the other, so a matching
   background with a mismatching face (or the reverse) is the classic
   signature.
4. **SSCD** — cosine of the `sscd_disc_mixup` ONNX embeddings through
   onnxruntime-web (browser) / onnxruntime-node (harness), the same pinned
   artifact, preprocessing and match threshold (0.85) as the crawler's
   second matching layer. Only when `LAB_SSCD_MODEL_URL` is configured;
   otherwise the tool says so and scores with hashing only.
5. **Alignment + heat map** — the suspect is resampled onto the original
   through the eye-pair similarity transform (the E1 face-swap alignment:
   exact rotation, scale, translation), then both are compared on a 16x16
   tile grid; the share of tiles whose mean luma difference is within
   tolerance is shown as the highlighted matching regions. Without
   landmarks the tiles compare the unaligned pair (indicated honestly).
6. **Pixel identity** — byte-equal images short-circuit: pixel equality is
   the one claim that needs no inference.

Face landmarks in the browser come from @vladmandic/human (the E1 harness's
landmark tool), loaded from our own origin (see `scripts/copy-lab-assets.mjs`)
with model files from `LAB_HUMAN_MODEL_BASE` (default: the library author's
model host, the same place the E1 harness uses). Where the model cannot
load, the tool degrades to whole-image + tile scoring and says so.

### Thresholds and how they were chosen

Provisional until a real E1/E3 run recalibrates them; every value is a named
constant in `score.ts` pinned by unit tests:

| Signal | Strong | Match | Borderline/weak | Not a match | Chosen because |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PDQ distance (any region) | d ≤ 12 | d ≤ 24 | d ≤ 31 | d > 31 | production matches at d < 31 (`PDQ_MATCH_THRESHOLD`); the crawler treats 25–31 as borderline; d ≤ 12 is the well-inside band the E1 synthetic runs showed for re-encodes |
| PDQ quality floor | — | — | — | quality < 25 = unreliable (0 points) | flat or blurred images hash alike without meaning anything; E1 uses 50 for its production-shape baseline, the trace tool accepts lower-quality evidence but never from degenerate input |
| SSCD cosine | ≥ 0.93 | ≥ 0.85 | ≥ 0.75 weak | < 0.75 | 0.85 is the crawler's `SSCD_THRESHOLD`; 0.93/0.75 bracket it |
| Tile agreement | ≥ 0.85 | ≥ 0.6 | ≥ 0.45 weak | < 0.45 | a swapped face or a shifted crop costs a bounded share of tiles; unrelated images sit far below |
| Verdict | strong: ≥ 3.5 points AND at least one strong signal | possible: ≥ 1.5 points | — | none | strong needs agreement across at least two independent measures (e.g. background + tiles + SSCD); one clear region alone stays possible; pixel-identical images are strong outright |

Evidence points: strong 2, match 1, borderline/weak 0.5, none/unreliable 0.
The face score contributes evidence only when it matches (her face carried
into the morph); a mismatching face never subtracts — with the background
matching, that mismatch IS the morph signature.

### The metric (charter)

On the E1 test set, the right source is found 95% of the time with at most
1% wrong-source matches. `tools/lab-bench trace <set-dir>` measures both:
every probe is scored against every gallery original, candidates ranked by
(evidence points, SSCD cosine, whole-image distance), and the run reports the
share of probes whose right source ranks first plus the share of
probe-vs-wrong-original pairs that reach the strong verdict.

## Deployment notes

- The tool is behind `ENABLE_LAB` like the rest of the Lab, and the page
  says when it is off.
- The evidence PDF is generated in the browser with pdf-lib (the notices'
  library; it is isomorphic) and is English-only (standard fonts cannot
  draw Devanagari); it is a standalone research document, not part of
  complaint filing.
- The onnxruntime-web WASM engine and the landmark library's browser build
  are copied from `node_modules` into `public/ort` and `public/human` at
  install time (`scripts/copy-lab-assets.mjs`), served same-origin, and
  never committed. The SSCD weights are never committed; `LAB_SSCD_MODEL_URL`
  points at the owner's copy, and the CSP for `/lab/trace` admits only the
  configured model origins.
