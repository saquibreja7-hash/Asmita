# NGO Network Announcement Draft

Status: draft, pending founder/legal review and NGO partner approval.

Subject: Asmita: privacy-first support for adults facing non-consensual intimate image abuse

Hello,

Asmita is being prepared as a privacy-first support platform for adults in India who are dealing with non-consensual intimate image abuse. The platform is designed to help victims submit links as text, avoid uploading intimate media, prepare platform-specific notices after legal review, and track progress through a case dashboard.

The current build includes:

- Adult email OTP registration and age attestation.
- A hard minor pathway that routes minors to child-safety support before collecting email or URLs.
- Local-only URL parsing; the system does not open, preview, download, or fetch submitted content.
- Draft notice generation gated by legal review and human-verified platform contacts.
- Case tracking, audit trail, escalation scheduling, and 7-day support package generation.
- Support resource links including emergency, child-safety, counselling, and legal services directories.

We are seeking NGO input on:

- Victim support workflows.
- Safe referral and vouching processes.
- Language clarity in English and Hindi.
- Escalation and legal handoff safeguards.
- Beta readiness criteria.

This message is not a public launch announcement and does not request real victim cases yet. Real cases should begin only after legal sign-off, verified contacts, POCSO protocol approval, and beta partner onboarding are complete.
