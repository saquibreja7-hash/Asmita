# Human Review Required

The master TODO list forbids AI from inventing legal approvals, platform Grievance Officer contacts, final notice text, or POCSO reporting policy.

Current code intentionally keeps those areas gated:

- Platform contact values default to `<TO_BE_VERIFIED_BY_HUMAN>`.
- Notice templates are drafts with `reviewedByLegal: false`.
- Terms of use are marked pending legal review.
- The minor pathway gives referral information and does not collect URLs.
