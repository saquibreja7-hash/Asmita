# Code of Conduct

Asmita contributors are expected to keep the project safe, respectful, and survivor-centered.

## Expected Behavior

- Use respectful language.
- Assume sensitive context and avoid sensationalism.
- Protect privacy by default.
- Welcome careful questions and good-faith review.
- Keep criticism specific to code, documentation, process, or safety impact.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or abusive language.
- Sharing or requesting intimate media, real NCII links, or survivor-identifying information.
- Doxxing, stalking, or targeted intimidation.
- Sexualized comments, jokes, examples, or test data.
- Attempts to bypass safety, legal, or review gates.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, block users, or report abuse when needed to protect contributors, users, or survivors.

If you see harmful behavior, contact the maintainer privately where possible.
